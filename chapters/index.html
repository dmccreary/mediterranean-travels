    Chapters¶

    This guide is organized into 21 chapters covering 598 concepts. There is one chapter for each day of the trip, November 11–23, 2026, plus two chapters to read before we leave, two deep dives on Paul at Ephesus, two deep dives on the Oracle and the Algorithm, and one chapter for the trip home. Day 9 on Crete has two chapters, one for Knossos and one for Venetian Heraklion.

    Chapter Overview¶

    1. Before We Go, Part 1: The Long Timeline of the Eastern Mediterranean - The geography of the route and the long run of eras from the Bronze Age to the Ottomans, so every later chapter has a place on the timeline.
    2. Before We Go, Part 2: Traveling Well and the Learning for All Thread - How the trip fits together and how to travel well in the off-season, plus the start of the Learning for All thread on literacy, alphabets, and AI in education.
    3. Day 1: Istanbul, a City on Two Continents - Arrival day in Istanbul: how a Greek colony on the Bosphorus became Constantine's capital and then Mehmed's, plus a first Turkish breakfast and first Turkish words.
    4. Day 2: Byzantine Constantinople and Hagia Sophia - Justinian's city: the Hippodrome, Hagia Sophia, and the Basilica Cistern, with the skills of reading a ruin and the long story of the Orthodox Church, the schism, and the Fourth Crusade.
    5. Day 3: Ottoman Istanbul, Topkapı, and Friday Prayer - Topkapı Palace, Suleiman and Sinan, and the Süleymaniye complex on a Friday, with Islam in daily life and the charitable endowments that paid for free schools.
    6. Day 4: Bazaars, Scripts, and Presses - The Grand Bazaar and Spice Bazaar before we board the Viking Star, the multi-faith Ottoman city, and the long, contested arrival of printing in Istanbul.
    7. Day 5: The End of Empire and the Birth of Modern Turkey - Ottoman decline, Greek independence, Gallipoli, and Atatürk's republic, ending at Gülhane Park where the 1928 alphabet reform began.
    8. Day 6: Troy and Gallipoli - Two battlefields three thousand years apart: the mound of Hisarlik and Homer's Iliad, then the Gallipoli peninsula and how Turks and Anzacs remember 1915.
    9. Paul at Ephesus, Part 1: The Apostle's Life, Letters, and Ephesian Years - A deep dive on Paul of Tarsus: the Greek and Roman world he inherited, his life and journeys, which letters are really his, and his two to three years in Ephesus.
    10. Day 7: Ephesus, a Greco-Roman City - Walking Ephesus from Curetes Street to the Library of Celsus and the Great Theater, the Temple of Artemis and the silversmiths' riot, and the Christian sites around Selçuk.
    11. Paul at Ephesus, Part 2: Ideas, Legacy, and Lessons in Leading Change - Paul's central ideas and their long legacy, good and harmful, and what his movement teaches about leading schools from direct instruction to AI-native learning.
    12. Day 8: Rhodes, From the Colossus to the Knights - Hellenistic Rhodes and its school of rhetoric, the medieval town of the Knights Hospitaller, Lindos, and the Jewish Quarter whose community was deported in 1944.
    13. Day 9, Part 1: Knossos, the Minoans, and Linear B - Morning at Knossos and the Heraklion Archaeological Museum: Minoan palace culture, Evans' reconstructions, and how Linear B was deciphered.
    14. Day 9, Part 2: Venetian Heraklion and Crete Today - Afternoon in Venetian Heraklion: the Koules fortress and city walls, the Cretan Renaissance and El Greco, Cretan scholars and the Aldine press, and the Cretan diet and village life today.
    15. Day 10: Athens, the Acropolis, and the Persian Wars - Arrival at Piraeus and the Acropolis: the Parthenon and its sculptures, the Persian Wars that paid for them, and the Greek theater and its tragedies.
    16. Day 11: Athens, the Agora, Democracy, and Paul on the Areopagus - The Ancient Agora and the Pnyx, how Athenian democracy worked and whom it left out, Socrates on trial, the Stoics, and Paul's speech on the Areopagus.
    17. The Oracle and the Algorithm, Part 1: The Oracle - How the Oracle of Delphi worked, its famous answers and its flaws, the inscriptions 'Know thyself' and 'Nothing in excess,' and why it lasted twelve centuries and then fell silent.
    18. Day 12: Delphi - The day trip to Delphi in the oracle's own off-season: the Sacred Way and treasuries, the theater and stadium, the Charioteer, Athena Pronaia, and the villages that live beside the site.
    19. The Oracle and the Algorithm, Part 2: The Algorithm - What Delphi teaches about living with machines that predict: data, ambiguity, bias, self-fulfilling predictions, calibration, horizons, and consent, ending in a personal reflection.
    20. Day 13: Athens, Museums, Tutors, and the City Today - The National Archaeological Museum and the Antikythera mechanism, Aristotle's Lyceum and the case for one-on-one tutoring, and the everyday life of modern Athens.
    21. Going Home: Learning With Communities, Not To Them - A closing chapter for the trip home that ties the Learning for All stories together into lessons for spreading education with communities rather than to them.

    How to Use This Guide¶

    Read each day's chapter at breakfast or the night before. The deep-dive chapters have their own reading times: Paul Part 1 the evening of Day 6 and Part 2 the evening of Day 7; the Oracle the night of Day 11 and the Algorithm on the drive back from Delphi. Chapters follow the order of the trip, and every concept is introduced before any later chapter relies on it, so reading in order means nothing is used before it has been explained.

    Note: Each chapter includes a list of the concepts it covers. If a chapter refers back to an idea you skipped, its Prerequisites section shows which earlier chapter explains it.
