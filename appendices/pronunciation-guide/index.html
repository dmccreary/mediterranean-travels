    Pronunciation Guide¶

    Every hard-to-say name and word in this book, in one place. Each term also has a pronounce button where it is first used in the chapters. The respelling shows the stressed syllable in capitals.

    English means the usual English pronunciation of a name that has one, such as Ephesus. Local means the Turkish or modern Greek pronunciation, such as Kuşadası.
