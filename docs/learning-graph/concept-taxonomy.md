# Concept Taxonomy

Every concept in the learning graph belongs to exactly one of these fifteen
categories. The categories follow the shape of the trip: a foundation layer,
the historical eras from the Bronze Age to today, the food along the route,
and the three threads that run through the guide (leadership and change,
prediction and AI, and learning for all). The TaxonomyID is the short code
used in [learning-graph.csv](learning-graph.csv).

| Category | TaxonomyID | Concepts that belong here |
|---|---|---|
| Foundations and Timeline | FOUND | Geography of the region, the long timeline of periods, the big faiths and empires we assume the reader has heard of, and the skills of reading a ruin: classical orders, mosaics, frescoes, inscriptions, spolia, and stratigraphy |
| Thoughtful Travel and Etiquette | TRAV | The cruise and itinerary, off-season timing and weather, useful Turkish and Greek phrases, hospitality customs, etiquette, and what tourism means to the people who host us |
| Bronze Age Troy and Crete | BRONZ | Minoan Knossos, Linear A and B and their decipherment, Troy and its excavators, Homer and the Iliad, the Bronze Age collapse, and the alphabets that followed |
| Classical Greece and Athens | CLASS | The Acropolis and its buildings, the Agora, Athenian democracy, the Persian Wars, theater, and the philosophers from Socrates to Aristotle |
| Hellenistic and Roman World | HELRO | Alexander and Koine Greek, Hellenistic Rhodes and its Colossus, the Roman province of Asia, and the streets, theater, library, and houses of Ephesus |
| Paul and Early Christianity | PAUL | Paul's life, journeys, and letters; his years in Ephesus; his core ideas; his legacy, including the harmful parts; and the Christian sites around Ephesus |
| Byzantium and Constantinople | BYZ | Constantine, Justinian, Hagia Sophia, the cistern and walls, Orthodox icons, the schism, and the fall of Constantinople |
| Knights and Venetians | MEDV | The Crusades, the Knights Hospitaller in Rhodes, Venetian Crete and its fortresses, and the Cretan scholars and Venetian printers who spread Greek texts |
| Ottoman Empire and Islam | OTTO | Sultans and Sinan, Topkapı, the great mosques and their endowed complexes, the bazaars, the multi-faith empire, printing in Istanbul, and Islam in daily practice |
| Modern Turkey and Greece | MODRN | Greek independence, Gallipoli, Atatürk and the Republic, the 1923 population exchange, the Jews of Rhodes, World War II, and daily life in each place today |
| Food and Drink | FOOD | Turkish, Greek, and Cretan dishes and drinks, and the history carried in each one |
| Delphi and the Oracle | DELPH | The sanctuary and its monuments, how a consultation worked, the famous answers, the oracle's flaws and decline, and the inscriptions "Know thyself" and "Nothing in excess" |
| Prediction and AI | PRED | Machine learning and prediction, and the parallels between Delphi and modern models: data, ambiguity, bias, trust, calibration, horizons, and consent |
| Leadership and Change | LEAD | Paul as a case study in change leadership and its lessons for moving schools from direct instruction to AI-native learning |
| Learning for All | LFA | The stories along the route of who got to learn and who was left out, and their lessons for bringing AI-powered education to every child |

See the [Taxonomy Distribution Report](taxonomy-distribution.md) for how many
concepts fall into each category.
