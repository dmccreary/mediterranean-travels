The audience is curious adults, so a grade level in the 10-13 range is
appropriate. Proper names of places and people raise the score.

## Scoring

Each definition is scored on precision, conciseness, distinctiveness and
non-circularity (25 points each), and checked for business rules. The
automatic checks above cover length, circularity and cross-references;
precision and distinctiveness were reviewed by sampling.

## Recommendations
- The automatic circularity check flagged 66 terms because every key word of the label also appears in the definition. A sample review found these are compound names explained by their parts (for example, Olive Oil is "the oil pressed from the fruit of the olive tree"), not true circular definitions.
