## Critical Rule

**This image must contain absolutely no text of any kind: no title, no heading,
no labels, no letters, no numbers, no digits, no arrows, no callout lines, no
scale bars, no signature and no watermark.** Menus, bottles, jars, cups, cloths,
and napkins must show only blank surfaces or plain simple patterns that are
clearly not writing. No Greek, Turkish, or Arabic lettering of any kind. The
dish names are added later as an interactive HTML overlay, and any text in the
picture would clash with it.

## Image Specifications

- **Format**: PNG
- **Dimensions**: 1536 × 1024 px (landscape, 3:2)
- **Background**: plain, flat, warm parchment cream (#F5EFE3) showing only in the
  16 px margin around the table, with no texture, scenery, or vignette
- **Style**: a clean, flat editorial illustration with soft shading and crisp
  outlines, the same hand across the whole table. Not photorealistic. Suitable
  for an adult travel-history textbook. Warm, friendly, and calm.

The two halves must look like **near twins**. Draw each pair of dishes in the

stands next to the cup. Above the runner (the Turkish half) there is no glass of

## Layout Notes (important for the interactive overlay)

- **Leave the top 12% of the table (y about 1.6% to 13.2% of the image) completely
  plain**: only the flat oat-colored linen cloth, with nothing on it. Interactive
  name labels will be placed in this band, so no dish, plate edge, sprig, or
  shadow may reach into it. Keep the runner out of this band too.
- Keep every dish inside its own column. The only thing that crosses the columns
  is the runner and the cloth.
- Keep the dishes above and below the runner at matching distances from it, and
  keep the cloth and the runner identical on both halves.
- Do not draw menus, signs, paper napkins with patterns, or ribbons that look
  like they should hold text.
- Do not draw national flags, religious symbols, or any national colors as a
  theme.
- Once more: **no text, letters, numbers, labels, or marks of any kind** anywhere
  in the image.
