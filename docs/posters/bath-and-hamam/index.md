## About This MicroSim

A visitor may well book a hamam in Istanbul, and the chapter reads the bath as
a story that runs from Rome to the Ottomans. This poster stacks two cutaway
floor plans on the same footprint: a Roman bath above, with its changing
room, cold, warm, and hot rooms, and the hypocaust under the floors, and a
Turkish hamam below, with its cool hall, warm room, and hot steam room.
Reading down a column compares one stage of the bath in the two cultures.

The claim the chapter makes is modest. The hamam is descended from the Roman
and Byzantine baths and adapted to a new culture, not copied from them. In the
lower plan the cool hall runs across the first two columns, because the
hamam has three main rooms and the Roman bath four. That is the first place the
parallel breaks, and each zone says where it breaks again.

## The Five Columns

1. **Changing Room**: where both baths begin, and where the hamam's cool hall also serves as a place to rest.
2. **Cold Room**: the Roman cold room, and the second half of the hamam's cool hall.
3. **Warm Room**: the middle step of both baths.
4. **Hot Room**: the Roman hot room, and the hamam's steam room with its heated central stone.
5. **Heat Source**: the furnace, with the Roman hypocaust under the floors.

## How to Use

**Explore mode** (the default):

1. **Hover** over a column to see a short tooltip with its name and its idea.
2. **Click** a column to open an infobox below the image with its details. On a
   tablet or phone, tap a column instead.

**Quiz mode:** press **Quiz Mode** at the bottom. The name pills disappear, and
a question appears below the image. Click the column that answers it. A correct
answer shows a short explanation; a wrong one shakes the column so you can try
again. The score counts your correct answers, and **Try Again** starts a new
round with the questions in a new order.

**Edit mode:** add `?edit=true` to the address of `main.html` to drag the
corners of each zone, then press **Copy JSON** and paste the zones into
`data.json`.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

The learner will compare the rooms of a Roman bath with the rooms of a Turkish hamam, and for each of five stages state what the two share, where the match breaks, and why the hamam is best described as adapted from the Roman and Byzantine baths, not copied from them.

### Grade Level

Adult travelers on the trip (Mediterranean Travels).

### Duration

10–15 minutes

### Prerequisites

The two bath sections of [Chapter 6](../../chapters/06-bazaars-scripts-presses/index.md).

### Activities

1. **Exploration** (5 min): Hover over all five columns, then click each one and
   read its infobox. For each, find the line that says where the parallel breaks.
2. **Guided Practice** (5 min): Walk the upper plan from left to right, then the
   lower one. Where does the hamam skip a step, and where does it add one?
3. **Assessment** (5 min): Switch to Quiz mode and answer all nine questions.

### Assessment

The learner answers at least 8 of the 9 quiz questions correctly on the first
try and can state, in one sentence, why "adapted from" is a better description
than "copied from".
