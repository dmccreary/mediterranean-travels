## About This MicroSim

Delphi is a steep hillside, and the sanctuary is scattered across it, so a plan helps. This view follows the climb: the Castalian Spring, the Sacred Way, the treasuries, the Athenian Treasury, the Polygonal Wall, the Temple of Apollo, the theater, and the stadium, with the round tholos at the Athena Pronaia sanctuary below the road.

Each marker says what a petitioner or an athlete did there and what is left to see. The picture is stylized: the real distances and heights are not drawn to scale.

## How to Use

**Explore mode** (the default): hover over a numbered marker or its label to
read about that part. The marker and its label light up together.

**Quiz mode:** press **Quiz**. The labels disappear and a description appears.
Click the marker that matches it. A correct answer scores a point; a wrong one
lets you try again. **Try Again** starts a new round.

**Edit mode:** add `?edit=true` to the address of `main.html` to drag the
markers, then press **Copy JSON** and paste the positions into `data.json`.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

The learner will name nine places on the Delphi hillside in the order a visitor climbs them, say what a petitioner or an athlete did at each, and say what survives to see today.

### Grade Level

Adult travelers on the trip (Mediterranean Travels).

### Duration

10–15 minutes

### Prerequisites

The sanctuary and the oracle, from [Chapter 17](../../chapters/17-oracle/index.md) and [Chapter 18](../../chapters/18-delphi/index.md).

### Activities

1. **Exploration** (5 min): Hover over the markers from 1 to 9 in order, as if
   climbing. For each, say what a petitioner did there.
2. **Guided Practice** (5 min): Find the three places an athlete would use, and
   the three a petitioner would use.
3. **Assessment** (5 min): Switch to Quiz mode and identify all nine places.

### Assessment

The learner identifies at least 8 of the 9 places from their descriptions on the first try and can order the climb from the spring to the stadium.
