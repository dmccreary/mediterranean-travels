## Critical Rule

**This image must contain absolutely no text of any kind: no title, no heading,
no labels, no letters, no numbers, no digits, no arrows, no callout lines, no
scale bars, no signature and no watermark.** Inscribed walls must show only
plain stone with faint irregular marks that are clearly not writing. The names
of the places and the title are added later as an interactive HTML overlay, and
any text in the picture would clash with it.

## Image Specifications

- **Format**: PNG
- **Dimensions**: 1536 × 1024 px (landscape, 3:2)
- **Background**: plain, flat, warm parchment cream (#F5EFE3) in the margins and
  in any gap between the hillside and the edge of the picture, with no texture
  or vignette
- **Style**: a clean, flat editorial illustration with soft shading and crisp
  outlines. Not photorealistic. Suitable for an adult travel-history textbook.
  People, where drawn, are tiny, simplified, and dignified.

## Overall Composition

One **hillside elevation** of the sanctuary at Delphi, seen from the south, from
the valley looking north up the slope, so that **east is on the right**. The
ground is a steep hillside that climbs from the **lower right** to the **upper
left**, built up in stepped stone terraces. The picture is a stylized, not-to-scale
view: buildings are drawn larger than life so they can be seen, and the slope is
squeezed, but the **order of things must be correct**, because the real site is
steep and scattered:

- the stadium is the **highest** thing, above the theater and **outside** the
  walled sanctuary;
- the theater is directly above the temple, inside the wall;
- the Castalian Spring is **east** of the walled sanctuary, at the foot of the
  gorge between the cliffs;
- the Athena Pronaia sanctuary is further **east** still and **below** a thin
  modern road.

Palette: sandy limestone and pale marble buildings, olive-green and dry-gold
hillside, gray-ochre cliffs, a dusty sky-blue shadow in the gorge, with a few
dark pines. The same line weight everywhere. Keep a clear parchment margin of
about 3% on every side.

## What to Draw

All positions are the center of the feature, as a percentage of image width from
the left and image height from the top.

### The cliffs (top and right)

Along the very top, from about 4% to 68% across and from the top margin down to
about **10% down**, a band of tall, pale **gray-ochre cliff face** with vertical
streaks (the Phaedriades). A second cliff stands at the upper right, from about
80% to 96% across and down to about 45% down. Between the two cliffs, from about
68% to 80% across, a **narrow cleft** drops from the top of the picture down to
the spring at about 47% down. The hillside below the cliffs is dry gold with
scattered dark pines.

### 1. The Castalian Spring (74% across, 47% down)

At the foot of the cleft, a **rock-cut fountain house**: a long stone basin with
steps in front and small empty niches carved into the rock face above it, with a
thin thread of water running down. At the lower edge of this feature, close by
the modern road, a second small **paved fountain court** with a stone basin.

### 2. The Sacred Way and the gate (60% across, 71% down)

The walled sanctuary is a stone-walled precinct, roughly rectangular and tilted
up the slope, spanning about **18% to 64% across and 20% to 76% down**. Its main
**gate** is at its **lower right (southeast) corner**, at about **60% across and
71% down**. Just outside the gate, at about 66% across and 76% down, is a small
paved square with a short row of slim columns (a Roman market square). From
the gate, a pale paved **zigzag road** goes west (left) along the lowest terrace
to about 30% across and 61% down, turns sharply back up and to the right along
the long wall to about 50% across and 51% down, and turns again up to the temple
terrace at about 52% across and 41% down. Draw three or four **tiny pilgrims**
in simple robes on the lower stretch of the road.

### 3. The treasuries (47% across, 66% down)

Along the lowest terrace, between about 38% and 56% across, a row of **five
small temple-shaped buildings** of pale stone, each a little different in size.
Two are complete with gabled fronts and a few columns; the others are only low
rectangular foundations. One of the complete ones has a porch held up by two
carved female figures instead of columns.

### 4. The Athenian Treasury (30% across, 58% down)

At the first sharp bend of the road, a **small, complete Doric temple front** of
pale marble with four short columns, a triangular gable, and a row of square
blank panels along the top. It stands out as the only complete small building
on the lower terraces. About 5% wide.

### 5. The polygonal wall (44% across, 49% down)

A long **retaining wall** running left to right across the middle of the
sanctuary, about 28% to 58% across and 46% to 54% down. Every stone is a
**many-sided irregular block** fitted tightly against its neighbors with no
mortar and no straight courses. Its face carries faint, shallow, irregular
scratches that are clearly not writing. A long, low, narrow porch stands against
its lower face at the right.

### 6. The temple terrace (50% across, 36% down)

On the terrace above the wall, a large **Doric temple** in pale stone, long and
low, with a gabled roof and a row of heavy, plain columns along its near side.
Six columns are standing; the rest of the temple is foundations. A **ramp** and
a large rectangular **stone altar** stand at its right (east) end. Near the
altar, a **tall bronze column of three intertwined snakes** (a copy of an old
monument) stands on a low round base. A small plain **beehive-shaped stone** stands beside the road
just below.

### 7. The theater (42% across, 26% down)

A **steep half-circle of curved stone seats** cut into the hillside directly
above the temple, about 10% wide, with a small flat stage in front. It looks down
over the temple.

### 8. The stadium (36% across, 13% down)

On the highest ledge of all, **above and outside the sanctuary wall**, just below
the cliff, a **long, narrow running track** seen from the side as a flat gold
strip about 14% wide, with a few rows of stone seats along its upper edge and
four small pillars at its right end. A short, steep path climbs up to it from
the theater.

### 9. Athena Pronaia and the Tholos (84% across, 80% down)

At the lower right, **below** a thin pale **modern road** that crosses the whole
slope from the left edge at about 84% down, to about 78% down at 60% across, to
about 62% down at the right edge, a long narrow terrace holds the Pronaia
sanctuary: a few low temple foundations and, at its center (84% across, 80%
down), a **round marble building**, the Tholos, with an outer ring of slim
columns, three of them taller than the rest, and a plain round inner wall. Below the terrace the ground drops steeply into a **deep river
gorge** with a thin ribbon of water and a gray-green plain of olive trees
beyond.

## Layout Notes (important for the interactive overlay)

- Keep the nine features at least about 8% apart. Leave open, calm space around
  each for the numbered markers.
- Draw no modern buildings other than the thin road: no cars, no signs, no
  village, no fences, no tourists.
- The Sacred Way must read as a **zigzag**: a long run left, a sharp turn, a long
  run right, a short climb to the temple.
- Do not draw frames, plaques, or ribbons that look like they should hold text.
- Once more: **no text, letters, numbers, labels, or marks of any kind**
  anywhere in the image.
