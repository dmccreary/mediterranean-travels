## About This MicroSim

Almost every stop on our route holds a story about who got to learn and who was
shut out. This poster puts five of those places in one row, in date order, drawn
at the same scale so that you can compare who is in the room, what the learners
hold, and how close the teacher stands.

Each classroom follows the four-step pattern from
[Chapter 2](../../chapters/02-traveling-well/index.md): learning is locked up, a
new technology or institution opens it, someone resists, and the circle widens
but never all the way. The five are not steps on a ladder. Several widened access
while others kept narrowing it, and every panel ends with the same question:
who was still left outside?

## How to Use

**Explore mode** (the default):

1. **Hover** over a classroom to see a short tooltip with its name and its idea.
2. **Click** a classroom to open an infobox below the image with the four steps
   and where we meet it on the route. On a tablet or phone, tap a classroom
   instead.

**Quiz mode:** press **Quiz Mode** at the bottom. The name pills disappear, and
a question appears below the image. Click the classroom that answers it. A
correct answer shows a short explanation; a wrong one shakes the classroom so you
can try again. The score counts your correct answers, and **Try Again** starts a
new round with the questions in a new order.

**Edit mode:** add `?edit=true` to the address of `main.html` to drag the
corners of each classroom's zone, then press **Copy JSON** and paste the zones
into `data.json`.

## The Five Classrooms

1. **The Lyceum**, about 335 BCE: Aristotle's research school in a public
   gymnasium, run by a foreigner who could not own land in Athens.
2. **Rhodes**, first century BCE: the school of rhetoric that Cicero sailed to,
   the old pattern of traveling to the teacher.
3. **A Venetian print shop**, about 1500: Aldus Manutius and the small, plain,
   portable book.
4. **A medrese at the Süleymaniye**, 1550s: free higher education paid for by an
   endowment.
5. **A learning room today**: one-to-one tutoring within reach of every child,
   with a person in the room.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

The learner will compare five classrooms from the route and, for each one, state
who was inside, what opened the door, and who was still left outside.

### Grade Level

Adult travelers on the trip (Mediterranean Travels).

### Duration

10–15 minutes

### Prerequisites

The four-step Learning for All pattern from
[Chapter 2](../../chapters/02-traveling-well/index.md).

### Activities

1. **Exploration** (5 min): Hover over all five classrooms, then click each one
   and read its infobox. For each, find the line that says who was left
   outside.
2. **Guided Practice** (5 min): Compare the medrese and the Aldine Press. Both
   cut the cost of learning. What did each one change, and who did each one
   still leave out?
3. **Assessment** (5 min): Switch to Quiz mode and answer all ten questions.

### Assessment

The learner answers at least 9 of the 10 quiz questions correctly on the first
try and can state, in one sentence, why the five classrooms are not a ladder of
progress.
