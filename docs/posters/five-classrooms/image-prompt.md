## Critical Rule

**This image must contain absolutely no text of any kind: no title, no heading,
no labels, no letters, no numbers, no digits, no arrows, no callout lines, no
scale bars, no signature and no watermark.** Scrolls, books, tablets, slates,
screens, signs, and banners must show only blank surfaces or soft abstract
squiggles that are clearly not writing. The classroom names are added later as
an interactive HTML overlay, and any text in the picture would clash with it.

## Image Specifications

- **Format**: PNG
- **Dimensions**: 1536 × 1024 px (landscape, 3:2)
- **Background**: plain, flat, warm parchment cream (#F5EFE3) showing only in the
  gaps between panels, with no texture, scenery, or vignette
- **Style**: a clean, flat editorial illustration with soft shading and crisp
  outlines, the same hand in every panel. Not photorealistic. Suitable for an
  adult travel-history textbook. People are drawn in a friendly, dignified,
  simplified way, with no caricature.

## Overall Composition

Five tall panels side by side in one row, each **288 px wide and 992 px tall**,
separated by 16 px parchment gaps, with a 16 px outer margin. Each panel is one
classroom from a different age. All five are drawn **from the same camera
height, at the same scale, and in the same flat side-on view**: the floor or
ground line sits at the same height in every panel (about 90% of the way down
the image), and the teacher in each panel is about the same size. A viewer
should be able to look across the row and compare who is in the room, what the
learners hold, and how close the teacher stands.

Each panel has its own soft color mood, so the five read as separate places,
but the same line weight and shading ties them together. From left to right the
panels are numbered 1 to 5 below, in order of date.

## What to Draw

### Panel 1 (far left): The Lyceum, about 335 BCE

**Mood**: sage green and olive, with warm limestone.
**Visual**: the shaded, covered walkway of an Athenian gymnasium, with a row of
plain columns on one side and plane trees behind. A teacher in a simple cloak
walks slowly along the walkway, talking, followed by a small group of five or
six men who listen and gesture. Behind them is a sandy wrestling ground. At one
side, a low bench holds open scrolls and pressed plant specimens, and a clay
bowl holds a small animal skeleton. The mood is outdoor, informal, curious, and
busy with collecting, not a lecture from a platform.

### Panel 2: Rhodes, the first century BCE

**Mood**: sea blue and white marble.
**Visual**: a bright columned hall open to the harbor. In the middle, a young
Roman in a white toga with a purple edge stands and delivers a speech, one arm
raised. A Greek teacher in a plain cloak stands close beside him, head tilted,
listening closely and holding up one finger to stop him. A few other young
Romans sit on marble benches. Through the columns, a harbor with a Roman ship
at the quay and sea beyond. A stack of wax tablets, blank, lies on a bench.

### Panel 3: A Venetian print shop, about 1500

**Mood**: warm amber, ink brown, and candlelight.
**Visual**: a cramped workshop. On the left, a wooden screw press with a worker
pulling the bar. In the middle, a man at a slanted type case setting small
metal type. Along the top, sheets of paper hang on lines to dry. On a table in
front, a neat stack of **tiny octavo books** bound in brown leather, each small
enough to hold in one hand. At the right, a bearded scholar in a dark coat
reads one of them while another man tucks one into his coat pocket. On the back
wall, a painted sign shows a dolphin wrapped around an anchor, with no words.

### Panel 4: A medrese at the Süleymaniye, the 1550s

**Mood**: turquoise, terracotta, and cream, with a pale blue sky.
**Visual**: an arcaded courtyard of small domed rooms around a fountain. Through
one arched opening on the right, a larger domed classroom where a teacher in a
turban and long robe sits on a cushion before a half circle of seated students.
In the courtyard, a boy in a plain robe carries a covered dish and a loaf of
bread from the direction of the kitchen toward a student's cell. At the left
edge, under a covered corner, a few small children, both boys and girls, sit
at a low desk with a teacher, as at a neighborhood primary school.

### Panel 5 (far right): A learning room today

**Mood**: soft white, pale teal, and a few warm accents.
**Visual**: a bright, plain community room with large windows. In the
foreground, a woman in everyday clothes sits close beside a girl of about ten.
The girl holds a tablet, and the woman leans in, looking at the girl, not the
screen. The screen shows only a soft abstract glow. Behind them, several other
children work at their own tables on tablets, with two more adults moving
between the tables, bending down to talk. A plant on a windowsill. No logos, no
robots, and no recognizable product screens.

## Layout Notes (important for the interactive overlay)

- **Leave the top 12% of each panel completely plain**: only the flat mood color
  of that panel, with nothing in it. Interactive name labels will be placed in
  this band, so no tree, dome, column, sign, or head may reach into it.
- Keep every panel's floor or ground line at the same height, and every
  teacher's head at about the same height, across the whole row.
- Keep all figures and objects inside their own panel. Nothing may cross a gap.
- Draw each teacher and learner large enough to read as a person, with a clear
  face and hands. The panels are narrow, so stack the scene toward the
  foreground rather than shrinking the people.
- Do not draw frames, plaques, or ribbons that look like they should hold text.
- Once more: **no text, letters, numbers, labels, or marks of any kind** anywhere
  in the image.
