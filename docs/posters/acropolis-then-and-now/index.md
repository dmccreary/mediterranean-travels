## About This MicroSim

Nearly everyone on the trip stands on the rock on
[Day 10](../../chapters/15-athens-acropolis/index.md). This poster draws the same
aerial view twice: the Acropolis as it stood about 406 BCE, with painted
sculpture and a bronze Athena, and the ruin a visitor climbs today. The five
monuments sit in the same place in both panels, in the order a visitor meets
them: the path and gateway, the temple of Athena Nike, the Parthenon, the
Erechtheion, and the open-air statue.

The questions the picture asks are Chapter 4's reading-a-ruin questions: what
was added, what was taken away, and what was restored, and what did the
restorers choose to show? The later layers (an explosion in 1687, the clearing
of the rock in the 1830s, restoration since 1975) read as changes to one rock.

## The Two Panels

1. **Late 5th Century BCE**: the rock as rebuilt under Pericles, with painted
   sculpture, the Propylaea gateway, and a bronze statue of Athena in the open air.
2. **Today**: a restored ruin with scaffolding, visitors, and the later
   layers cleared away.

Where the Parthenon Marbles belong is a live public argument, and it is not
settled here. See the companion MicroSim on separating agreed fact from position.

## How to Use

**Explore mode** (the default):

1. **Hover** over a panel to see a short tooltip with its name and its idea.
2. **Click** a panel to open an infobox below the image with its details. On a
   tablet or phone, tap a panel instead.

**Quiz mode:** press **Quiz Mode** at the bottom. The name pills disappear, and
a question appears below the image. Click the panel that answers it. A correct
answer shows a short explanation; a wrong one shakes the panel so you can try
again. The score counts your correct answers, and **Try Again** starts a new
round with the questions in a new order.

**Edit mode:** add `?edit=true` to the address of `main.html` to drag the
corners of each zone, then press **Copy JSON** and paste the zones into
`data.json`.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

The learner will compare the Acropolis of about 406 BCE with the rock a visitor climbs today, and for each of five monuments name what has been lost, rebuilt, replaced, or removed on purpose.

### Grade Level

Adult travelers on the trip (Mediterranean Travels).

### Duration

10–15 minutes

### Prerequisites

The Acropolis and the Parthenon, from [Chapter 15](../../chapters/15-athens-acropolis/index.md).

### Activities

1. **Exploration** (5 min): Hover over both panels, click each one, and read its
   infobox. Follow one monument, such as the Erechtheion, across both panels.
2. **Guided Practice** (5 min): Pick one change on the rock and decide which kind
   it was: lost, rebuilt, replaced, or removed on purpose. Say how you know.
3. **Assessment** (5 min): Switch to Quiz mode and answer all ten questions.

### Assessment

The learner answers at least 9 of the 10 quiz questions correctly on the first
try and can say, for the Parthenon, what is original, what is new marble, and
what is gone.
