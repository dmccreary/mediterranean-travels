## Critical Rule

**This image must contain absolutely no text of any kind: no title, no heading,
no labels, no letters, no numbers, no digits, no arrows, no callout lines, no
scale bars, no signature and no watermark.** Signs, banners, flags, and
inscriptions must show only blank surfaces, plain stripes, or soft abstract
squiggles that are clearly not writing, and never any Greek lettering. The
panel names are added later as an interactive HTML overlay, and any text in the

2. **The small temple of Athena Nike**: a very small four-column temple on a

3. **The Parthenon**: the largest building, a long white marble temple with a

4. **The Erechtheion**: a smaller, odd-shaped temple with porches at different

5. **The statue between the gateway and the Parthenon**: in the center, behind

## What to Draw

### Panel 1 (left): the late 5th century BCE

**Mood**: fresh honey-white marble, warm light, a pale blue sky.
**Visual**: the rock newly rebuilt, with every building complete and bright.
The Parthenon has its full roof, its pediments with figures in the triangles at
each end, a band of square panels above the columns, and a continuous carved
band around the inner building, with 8 columns on the ends and 17 on the sides.
Color appears only as **soft, partial, muted accents on the sculpted parts**:
dusty blue and warm red on the pediment figures, the square panels, and the
carved band, and a little bronze-gold on the roof edge. The columns and walls
stay plain marble. The tall Athena statue is drawn in dark bronze, with a
spear that rises high and a shield on her arm. A line of small figures in
simple robes climbs the ramp in a procession, carrying a folded cloth, with a
few cattle behind. The scene around the Parthenon holds an altar in the open
at its east end and a few small figures. No scaffolding, no crowd, and no modern
features. The houses below are low and plain.

### Panel 2 (right): today

**Mood**: weathered honey-gray marble, the same light, the same sky.
**Visual**: the same rock and the same five places, now a restored ruin.
The Parthenon has **no roof**, and the **middle of both long sides is gone**,
so the building is two separate masses, the western end and the eastern end,
with a gap between and a few columns still standing at the sides. The pediments
are mostly empty. New, brighter white marble blocks appear as patches among
the old, weathered, darker stone. A **small light scaffold** stands against
the west front, and a **small crane** with a thin arm stands at the east end of
the building. Beside the gateway, the small temple stands complete but with
bright new patches. The Erechtheion is intact, with six slightly pale statues
on its porch. The olive tree is small and young. The square base in the
center is empty. **A few small groups of visitors in everyday clothes**, with
hats and daypacks, climb the ramp and stand on the summit, and a guide holds
up a plain blank paddle. At the foot of the slope at lower right, a curved
Roman theater built into the hillside with arched stone walls. At the bottom
center, a small old arched gate at the start of the ramp. At the far east end
of the rock, one flagpole with a plain blue-and-white striped flag. The houses
below are a dense modern city of flat roofs. No minaret, no mosque, no
medieval tower, and no hotel or sign anywhere on the rock.
