## Critical Rule

**This image must contain absolutely no text of any kind: no title, no heading,
no labels, no letters, no numbers, no digits, no arrows, no callout lines, no
scale bars, no signature and no watermark.** Screens, scrolls, tablets, signs,
and banners must show only blank surfaces or soft abstract squiggles that are
clearly not writing. The tile names are added later as an interactive HTML
overlay, and any text in the picture would clash with it.

## Image Specifications

- **Format**: PNG
- **Dimensions**: 1536 × 1024 px (landscape, 3:2)
- **Background**: plain, flat, warm parchment cream (#F5EFE3) showing only in the
  gaps between tiles, with no texture, scenery, or vignette
- **Style**: a clean, flat editorial illustration with soft shading and crisp
  outlines, the same hand and palette in every tile. Not photorealistic.
  Suitable for an adult travel-history textbook. People are drawn in a friendly,
  dignified, simplified way; no caricature.

## Overall Composition

Six equal square-ish tiles in a grid of **3 columns by 2 rows**, separated by
parchment gaps. Every tile is a small diptych: a thin vertical gold seam
(#C9A227) splits it into a **left half** and a **right half** of equal width.

- **Left half = ancient Delphi.** Warm palette: sun-baked limestone (#E8DCC2),
  terracotta (#B5532D), olive gold (#C9A227), deep bronze (#7A5A2B).
- **Right half = today's algorithm.** Cool palette: soft teal (#1F6F8B), pale
  mist (#E4EEF1), graphite (#2A2E3A), with a few warm accents so the halves
  feel related.

The two halves of a tile show the **same human situation** in two eras, drawn
at the same scale and from the same camera height, so a viewer can read across
the seam. From the left, the tiles in reading order are numbered 1 to 6 below.

## What to Draw

### Tile 1 (top row, left): Who asks

**Left half**: a traveler in a plain wool cloak and belted tunic climbs a
stone path toward a temple terrace, leading a small goat on a cord and holding a
coin pouch. Two other petitioners wait behind him.
**Right half**: a person in everyday clothes sits at a desk typing at a laptop,
a phone beside it. Behind her, faint pale silhouettes of many other people
stand in rows against the wall, their faces turned away, as people who are
predicted about without being asked.

### Tile 2 (top row, middle): Where the knowledge comes from

**Left half**: the gate of a sanctuary. Envoys from different lands, each in
distinct dress, arrive on foot and by mule: one in a long Lydian robe, one in
a short island tunic, one in a Spartan red cloak. Each carries a bundle or a
blank scroll. They talk with a priest at the gate.
**Right half**: a hall of server racks with soft teal lights. A river of blank
pale sheets flows from the left edge of the half into a glowing cube at the
center. A few bright strands in the stream, and many thin ones.

### Tile 3 (top row, right): The form of the answer

**Left half**: the Pythia, a calm woman in a long belted robe, sits on a tall
bronze tripod with a laurel branch in one hand, looking into a shallow bowl in
the other. Curling smoke rises beside her. A priest stands to her right with a
blank wax tablet. She is calm, never raving.
**Right half**: a large soft-edged speech panel floats over a desk, filled with
clean gray lines of placeholder squiggles (not letters). Beneath the lines are
three short horizontal bars of different lengths, like a simple probability
gauge, with no digits.

### Tile 4 (bottom row, left): Who interprets

**Left half**: the Athenian Assembly on a stepped hillside under a clear sky.
Citizens sit on the rock. One man stands on a low stone platform with an arm
raised, speaking. Many hands are raised. In the distance, a harbor with ships.
**Right half**: a small office. One person sits at a desk looking at a monitor
and holds a pen over a blank form. A colleague stands beside her with a hand
raised, as if in disagreement. A big green button on the desk is clearly
visible but untouched.

### Tile 5 (bottom row, middle): How we mishear

**Left half**: a king in a rich purple and gold robe and a gold circlet sits
on a throne, a satisfied smile on his face, one hand cupped behind his ear. A

larger and half hidden in shadow, the outline of a second, bigger walled city.
**Right half**: a person in a cozy chair holds a phone, nodding and smiling.
On the phone's screen, a soft glowing shape is the exact mirror image of the
shape on her own sweater. No text.

### Tile 6 (bottom row, right): When the answer changes the future

**Left half**: a country crossroads where three roads meet in the hills. A
young man on foot and an old man in a small carriage face each other at the
fork. The road behind the young man curves back around and rejoins the road
ahead of him, forming a loop.
**Right half**: a bird's-eye view of a cloverleaf highway interchange, with
small cars driving around its loops. A row of cars has already left the main
road early, onto a side road. Calm, evening light.

## Layout Notes (important for the interactive overlay)

- **Leave the top 12% of each tile completely plain**: only the flat background
  color of that half (limestone on the left, mist on the right). Interactive
  name labels will be placed in this band, so no figure or object may reach into
  it. Keep the seam line running all the way through it.
- Tiles are **480 px wide and 476 px tall**. The outer margin is 24 px. The
  gaps between tiles are 24 px, and are parchment cream.
- Keep every figure's feet and the ground line near the same height across the
  tiles of a row, so the rows read as bands.
- Keep the seam in the exact center of each tile (at 240 px from a tile's left
  edge), and do not let any object cross it, apart from the loop road in Tile
  6, which may touch it.
- No tile may contain a title, caption, or number. Do not draw frames or ribbons
  that look like they should hold text.
- Once more: **no text, letters, numbers, labels, or marks of any kind** anywhere
  in the image.
