## About This MicroSim

Chapters [6](../../chapters/06-bazaars-scripts-presses/index.md) and [14](../../chapters/14-venetian-heraklion/index.md) tell how a hand-copied book gave way to the small printed book that Aldus Manutius made in Venice. This poster sets the two side by side in six matched tiles: the writing surface, the hand or the type, the page and its size, who made it, how long it took, and who could own one.

Each tile says where the comparison stops being fair. A scribe is cheaper for one copy and a press for a hundred, and Aldus widened the circle of readers without opening it to everyone. All the text in the pictures is blurred on purpose, so the poster shows the form of a page, not its words.

## How to Use

**Explore mode** (the default):

1. **Hover** over a zone to see a short tooltip with its name and its idea.
2. **Click** a zone to open an infobox below the image with its details. On a
   tablet or phone, tap a zone instead.

**Quiz mode:** press **Quiz Mode** at the bottom. The name pills disappear, and
a question appears below the image. Click the zone that answers it. A correct
answer shows a short explanation; a wrong one shakes the zone so you can try
again. The score counts your correct answers, and **Try Again** starts a new
round with the questions in a new order.

**Edit mode:** add `?edit=true` to the address of `main.html` to drag the
corners of each zone, then press **Copy JSON** and paste the zones into
`data.json`.

## The Zones

1. **Writing Surface**: A Byzantine page was parchment from animal skin, an Aldine page was handmade paper, and paper was still one of the biggest costs of a printed book.
2. **Hand or Type**: A scribe's hand and Aldus's italic type were both writing, and the type was modeled on the hand.
3. **Page and Size**: Aldus made a classic small enough for a coat pocket through two choices: a small fold and a dense, plain page.
4. **Who Made It**: A scribe's book was one skilled hand's work. An Aldine book was the work of many hands, and each depended on the copyists before.
5. **How Long**: For one copy the scribe is cheaper. For a hundred copies the press wins overwhelmingly, because most of its cost is paid before the first page.
6. **Who Could Own**: Aldus widened the circle, but only to men who had been to school, and he built a gate of his own.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

The learner will compare a hand-copied Byzantine page with a small Aldine page along six features (surface, hand or type, page and size, who made it, how long it took, and who could own one), and for each pair name where the comparison stops being fair.

### Grade Level

Adult travelers on the trip (Mediterranean Travels).

### Duration

10–15 minutes

### Prerequisites

Scribes, presses, and Aldine editions, from [Chapter 6](../../chapters/06-bazaars-scripts-presses/index.md) and [Chapter 14](../../chapters/14-venetian-heraklion/index.md).

### Activities

1. **Exploration** (5 min): Hover over every zone, then click each one and read
   its infobox.
2. **Guided Practice** (5 min): Pick two tiles. For each, say which book is cheaper to make for one copy and which for a hundred, and why.
3. **Assessment** (5 min): Switch to Quiz mode and answer all 10 questions.

### Assessment

The learner answers at least 9 of the 10 quiz questions correctly on the first
try and can state, for any one tile, where the comparison stops being fair.
