## Critical Rule

**This image must contain absolutely no text of any kind: no title, no heading,
no labels, no letters, no numbers, no digits, no arrows, no callout lines, no
scale bars, no signature and no watermark.** This is a picture of books, so it
matters most here: **every page, scroll, tablet, sign, and banner must show
writing only as soft, blurred rows of gray-brown bars, like out-of-focus lines
of text, with no letterform of any kind in any alphabet.** Never draw Greek,
Latin, Arabic, or Turkish letters, not even decorative ones. A decorated
initial is drawn as an abstract square of gold, red, and blue pattern, not as
a letter. The feature names are added later as an interactive HTML overlay,
and any text in the picture would clash with it.

## Overall Composition

Six equal tiles in a grid of **3 columns by 2 rows**, each **480 px wide and
476 px tall**, with a 24 px outer margin and 24 px parchment gaps. Every tile is
a small diptych like an open book: a thin vertical gold seam (#C9A227), drawn
like the gutter of a book, runs down the exact center of the tile (240 px from
its left edge) and splits it into a **left half** and a **right half** of equal
width.

- **Left half = the scribe's page.** A Byzantine monastery workshop, about the
  9th to 12th centuries. Warm palette: honey parchment (#E8D5A8), oxblood
  (#7A2E2E), ink brown (#4A3728), and old gold.
- **Right half = the Aldine page.** A Venetian print shop, about 1500. Cool
  palette: clean off-white paper (#F4F1EA), Venetian blue (#2F5D7C), graphite
  (#2A2E3A), with a few warm accents so the halves feel related.

The two halves of a tile show **the same feature of a book** in two ways,
drawn at the same scale and from the same camera height, so a viewer can read
across the seam. In reading order the tiles are numbered 1 to 6 below.

**Right half**: a close view of a printer's hand placing tiny metal blocks into

### Tile 3 (top row, right): the page and its size

**Left half**: a very large open book on a sloped wooden stand, as wide as the

**Right half**: a busy Venetian print shop. A scholar in a long coat and soft

**Right half**: the same room as a print shop. A pressman pulls a press bar,

- Tiles are **480 px wide and 476 px tall**, with the seam at the exact

- Every page, sheet, and sign shows only blurred gray bars, never letters. No
  tile may contain a title, caption, or number. Do not draw frames, plaques,
