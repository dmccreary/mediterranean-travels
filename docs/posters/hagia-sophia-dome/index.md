## About This MicroSim

The dome of Hagia Sophia, about 31 m across and about 55 m high, is the structure that made the building famous. This cutaway shows how it stands: four piers, four pendentives, a ring of 40 windows, the half-domes, and the buttresses that hold it. It also marks the collapse of the first dome in 558 and its rebuilding in 562, and the minarets the Ottomans added outside.

It is the companion to [Hagia Sophia: One Building, Four Lives](../hagia-sophia-four-lives/index.md): that poster shows what happened to the building, and this one shows how it stays up. The chapter does not say which buttresses are Byzantine and which are Sinan's, so the drawing does not claim to.

## How to Use

**Explore mode** (the default): hover over a numbered marker or its label to
read about that part. The marker and its label light up together.

**Quiz mode:** press **Quiz**. The labels disappear and a description appears.
Click the marker that matches it. A correct answer scores a point; a wrong one
lets you try again. **Try Again** starts a new round.

**Edit mode:** add `?edit=true` to the address of `main.html` to drag the
markers, then press **Copy JSON** and paste the positions into `data.json`.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

The learner will name the parts that hold up the Hagia Sophia dome, say what each one does, and tell which parts are Justinian's, which came after the collapse of 558, and which are later Ottoman additions.

### Grade Level

Adult travelers on the trip (Mediterranean Travels).

### Duration

10–15 minutes

### Prerequisites

The Hagia Sophia dome and Justinian, from [Chapter 4](../../chapters/04-byzantine-constantinople/index.md).

### Activities

1. **Exploration** (5 min): Hover over the markers from 1 to 8 and read what each part is.
2. **Guided Practice** (5 min): Follow the weight of the dome from the top to the ground, naming each part it passes through.
3. **Assessment** (5 min): Switch to Quiz mode and identify all 8 parts.

### Assessment

The learner identifies at least 7 of the 8 parts from their descriptions on the first try and can say which part turns a square room into a round base for a dome.
