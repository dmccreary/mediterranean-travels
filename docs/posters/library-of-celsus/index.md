## About This MicroSim

The front of the Library of Celsus is the best-known view in Ephesus. This picture reads it part by part: the steps and statue bases, the two storeys of columns with their Corinthian capitals, the niches with statues, the doorway to the reading room, and the burial chamber of Celsus under the building. It also marks the Gate of Mazaeus and Mithridates beside it.

It was a public library built as a son's memorial and a tomb, which makes it a stop in our Learning for All thread. Part of what we see was put back together in modern times, and marker 8 shows that as a drawing device, not as a map of which stones are new.

## How to Use

**Explore mode** (the default): hover over a numbered marker or its label to
read about that part. The marker and its label light up together.

**Quiz mode:** press **Quiz**. The labels disappear and a description appears.
Click the marker that matches it. A correct answer scores a point; a wrong one
lets you try again. **Try Again** starts a new round.

**Edit mode:** add `?edit=true` to the address of `main.html` to drag the
markers, then press **Copy JSON** and paste the positions into `data.json`.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

The learner will read the front of the Library of Celsus part by part: say which parts are original design, which are copies or reconstruction, and what the building was for, a public library built as a son's memorial and a tomb.

### Grade Level

Adult travelers on the trip (Mediterranean Travels).

### Duration

10–15 minutes

### Prerequisites

Curetes Street, Hadrian, and the classical orders, from [Chapter 10](../../chapters/10-ephesus-greco-roman-city/index.md).

### Activities

1. **Exploration** (5 min): Hover over the markers from 1 to 9 and read what each part is.
2. **Guided Practice** (5 min): Find the part that shows this was a tomb, and the part that shows it was a library.
3. **Assessment** (5 min): Switch to Quiz mode and identify all 9 parts.

### Assessment

The learner identifies at least 8 of the 9 parts from their descriptions on the first try and can say what the building was for and which parts were reassembled.
