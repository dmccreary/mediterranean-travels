## Critical Rule

**This image must contain absolutely no text of any kind: no title, no heading,
no labels, no letters, no numbers, no digits, no arrows, no callout lines, no
scale bars, no signature and no watermark.** Sails, banners, bases, plinths, and
the sides of buildings must show only blank surfaces or plain simple patterns
that are clearly not writing. No Greek lettering and no inscriptions. The tile
names are added later as an interactive HTML overlay, and any text in the
picture would clash with it.

## Image Specifications

- **Format**: PNG
- **Dimensions**: 1536 × 1024 px (landscape, 3:2)
- **Background**: plain, flat, warm parchment cream (#F5EFE3) showing only in the
  gaps between tiles and in the 16 px outer margin, with no texture, scenery, or
  vignette
- **Style**: a clean, flat editorial illustration with soft shading and crisp
  outlines, the same hand in every tile. Not photorealistic. Suitable for an
  adult travel-history textbook. People are drawn in a friendly, dignified,
  simplified way, with no caricature.

## Overall Composition

Six tiles in a grid of **2 columns by 3 rows**, each **744 px wide and 320 px
tall**, separated by 16 px parchment gaps, with a 16 px outer margin. The
**left column** is "what most people imagine". The **right column** is "what the
evidence supports". Each row asks the same question of both columns, and the two
tiles in a row are drawn **at the same scale and from the same camera**, so a
viewer can look across the row and compare.

- **Left column mood**: a warm, dramatic golden-orange sky and amber light, like a
  storybook engraving brought into color. The Colossus is drawn **solid, bold
  bronze** with strong shading.
- **Right column mood**: calm, clear daylight, a pale blue sky and soft white
  light. The Colossus, where it appears upright, is drawn as a **pale,
  translucent, soft-edged bronze figure**, like a reconstruction, to show that
  its exact look is a guess.

The statue is the Colossus of Rhodes: a giant bronze statue of Helios, the
Greek sun god, a muscular man in his prime with a calm face, short curled hair, a
**crown of sun rays** around his head, and a short cloth around his hips. Draw
the **same face, the same crown, and the same body** in every tile in which he
appears. Rows are numbered 1 to 3 from the top.

## What to Draw

### Row 1 (top, y about 1.6% to 32.8%): the figure

**Left tile (x about 1.0% to 49.5%):** a very tall bronze Helios seen from the
side-on view, **straddling the entrance of a harbor**, one giant foot on the
tip of a stone pier on the left and the other on the tip of a stone pier on the
right, the legs wide apart. One arm is raised toward the sky. Small ancient
sailing ships pass between the legs. A walled town of low flat-roofed buildings
sits behind the harbor. The figure's head comes to about 40 px below the top of
the tile.

**Right tile (x about 50.5% to 99.0%):** the **same giant Helios at exactly the
same height**, in the same side-on view, but **standing upright with his feet
together on a low plain stone platform on land**, arms hanging at his sides, near
the edge of a harbor. In pale, translucent, soft-edged bronze. Four tiny people
about 12 px tall stand at the foot of the platform for scale, and the same
walled town and harbor with two small ships sit behind him.

### Row 2 (middle, y about 34.4% to 65.6%): the site

Both tiles show **the same harbor, seen from high above at a slight angle**,
drawn at the same scale: a bay of calm turquoise water with a **wide harbor
entrance**, framed by two stone piers that reach out into the sea from the left
and the right. A few small ships sit inside the harbor. A walled town of
flat-roofed buildings rises on the land behind the harbor. The harbor entrance
is **wide**, about four times the width of the statue's footprint.

**Left tile:** a **giant bronze foot** (long, oval, a little wider at the toes)
stands at the tip of each of the two piers, and between the two feet a long,
**pale translucent bronze band** crosses the whole entrance over the water, to
show how far the legs would have to span.

**Right tile:** the **same pair of giant bronze feet** stand side by side on land,
on a low stone platform just inside the quay, close together, in pale,
translucent bronze. Higher up in the town, inside a walled court with a few
columns, a **second pair of faint, even paler footprints** marks another
possible spot. The two spots are clearly separated, with nothing connecting
them.

### Row 3 (bottom, y about 67.2% to 98.4%): the end

**Left tile:** a **long caravan of about eight camels** walks in a line along a
dusty road at the edge of a harbor, each camel loaded with large rough pieces of
bronze (a giant hand, a foot, a curved chunk of a leg) strapped on its back
under red cloth covers. A merchant in a long robe and head cloth leads the first
camel. A few more camels trail off into the distance. The town of Rhodes sits in
the distance. Golden-orange evening light.

**Right tile:** a calm daylight scene on level ground near the harbor wall. The
**broken bronze statue lies in large pieces** on the ground where it fell: a huge
torso, a head with its crown of rays, and a giant hand lying palm down, the
**thumb as thick as a tree trunk**. Cracked paving and a few tumbled blocks lie
around it, as after an earthquake. In the foreground, **three small visitors in
togas**, one of them a bearded man with a scroll under his arm, stand at the
thumb. Two of them stretch their arms around it, and their hands do not
reach. The scroll is blank.

## Layout Notes (important for the interactive overlay)

- **Leave the top 12% of each tile (about 38 px) completely plain**: only the flat
  sky color of that tile (golden-orange on the left, pale blue on the right), with
  nothing in it. Interactive name labels will be placed in this band, so no head,
  mast, tower, or crown may reach into it.
- Tiles are **744 px wide and 320 px tall**. The outer margin is 16 px. The gaps
  between tiles are 16 px and parchment cream. Left tiles start at x = 16 px
  and end at x = 760 px; right tiles start at x = 776 px and end at x = 1520 px.
  Rows start at y = 16, 352, and 688 px.
- Keep **every figure and object inside its own tile**. Nothing may cross a gap.
- In row 1, draw the two giant statues at **exactly the same height**, so the
  only difference between the tiles is the pose and the place.
- In row 2, keep the harbor, piers, and town identical in both tiles.
- Do not draw frames, plaques, inscriptions, or ribbons that look like they
  should hold text. The platform and the base show only plain stone.
- Do not draw scaffolding, cranes, or tools. Do not draw any modern objects.
- Do not draw any religious symbols other than the sun rays around Helios's head.
- Once more: **no text, letters, numbers, labels, or marks of any kind** anywhere
  in the image.
