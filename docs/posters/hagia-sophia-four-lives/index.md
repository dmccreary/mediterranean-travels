## About This MicroSim

Hagia Sophia is the clearest city of layers on the trip, and we visit it on [Day 2](../../chapters/04-byzantine-constantinople/index.md). This poster draws the same view of the nave four times, from the same camera: as Justinian's cathedral, as an Ottoman mosque after 1453, as a museum, and as a mosque again since 2020. A tall cross-section beside them shows what stayed through all four lives: the dome, the pendentives, and the ring of windows.

This is a living religious site and a politically contested one. The zones state the agreed dates and facts, and keep the argument over what the changes mean in clearly labeled contested-history notes, with each reading held sincerely.

## How to Use

**Explore mode** (the default):

1. **Hover** over a zone to see a short tooltip with its name and its idea.
2. **Click** a zone to open an infobox below the image with its details. On a
   tablet or phone, tap a zone instead.

**Quiz mode:** press **Quiz Mode** at the bottom. The name pills disappear, and
a question appears below the image. Click the zone that answers it. A correct
answer shows a short explanation; a wrong one shakes the zone so you can try
again. The score counts your correct answers, and **Try Again** starts a new
round with the questions in a new order.

**Edit mode:** add `?edit=true` to the address of `main.html` to drag the
corners of each zone, then press **Copy JSON** and paste the zones into
`data.json`.

## The Zones

1. **Cathedral**: For more than nine centuries the building was the seat of the Patriarch of Constantinople and the coronation church of emperors.
2. **Mosque**: Mehmed II made the cathedral a mosque in 1453, and nearly five centuries of Ottoman additions followed.
3. **Museum**: For 85 years the building was a museum, and the plastered-over Byzantine mosaics were uncovered and restored.
4. **Mosque Again**: Since July 2020 the building has been a mosque again, with the ground floor kept for worshippers and a route for visitors through the galleries.
5. **What Stayed**: The 562 dome on its four pendentives has been repaired and buttressed through all four lives, but not replaced.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

The learner will name the four lives of Hagia Sophia (cathedral, mosque, museum, mosque again), state for each one what was added, covered, or uncovered, and say which parts of the structure stayed through all four.

### Grade Level

Adult travelers on the trip (Mediterranean Travels).

### Duration

10–15 minutes

### Prerequisites

Layers of a city, spolia, and restoration, from [Chapter 4](../../chapters/04-byzantine-constantinople/index.md).

### Activities

1. **Exploration** (5 min): Hover over every zone, then click each one and read
   its infobox.
2. **Guided Practice** (5 min): Compare the cathedral and the museum tiles. What came back, and what stayed hidden? Then name two things the cross-section shows that no tile can.
3. **Assessment** (5 min): Switch to Quiz mode and answer all 10 questions.

### Assessment

The learner answers at least 9 of the 10 quiz questions correctly on the first
try and can name one thing added, one covered, and one uncovered across the four lives.
