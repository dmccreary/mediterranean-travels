## Critical Rule

**This image must contain absolutely no text of any kind: no title, no heading,
no labels, no letters, no numbers, no digits, no arrows, no callout lines, no
scale bars, no signature and no watermark.** Wall decoration must be abstract
looping curves, flowers, and geometric patterns that are clearly not writing:
**do not draw Arabic calligraphy, Quranic verses, or any lettering at all.**
Signs and banners are not part of the scene. The part names and the title are
added later as an interactive HTML overlay, and any text in the picture would
clash with it.

## Overall Composition

A **longitudinal cutaway** of a classic Ottoman mosque and its forecourt, as if
the building were sliced down its middle and we look into the cut. The courtyard
is on the **left**, the prayer hall is in the **center and right**, and the
qibla wall (the end wall of the hall) is at the **right**. The ground and floor
line sits at **88% of the way down** the image, with a thin darker floor slab
below it (88% to 91%). Everything is seen flat-on from the side, except the
right-hand end wall, which is turned very slightly toward the viewer so its face
and niche can be seen. Keep a clear parchment margin of about 3% on every side.

Palette: warm cream stone, lead-gray domes, terracotta roof accents, turquoise
and cobalt tile panels, and soft ochre carpet. The same line weight everywhere.
