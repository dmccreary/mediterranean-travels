## About This MicroSim

Knowing what a Turkish phrase means is not the same as knowing when to say
it. This MicroSim puts fourteen of the phrases from the chapter's table into
ten situations we will actually meet in Istanbul: walking into a café at 8 AM,
buying a simit, asking a price, paying for dinner, answering a shopkeeper's
welcome, greeting a fisherman at work, praising a home cook, getting past
someone on a tram, leaving a restaurant in the evening, and accepting more tea.

Each situation offers three phrases, each with its pronunciation guide. One
is the best choice; the other two are real Turkish that fits a different
moment. Most of the traps are phrases that belong to the other person in the
exchange: *Afiyet olsun* is what the cook says to us, and *Hoş geldiniz* is
what the shopkeeper says, not our reply.

After the tenth situation, the MicroSim shows a phrase sheet: the ten best
phrases with their pronunciations and meanings, which can be kept for the trip.

## How to Use

1. Read the situation and say the three phrases aloud, using the
   pronunciation guide under each one.
2. Choose a phrase (or press 1, 2 or 3). Each situation has one attempt. The
   best phrase turns green, a wrong choice red, and the panel gives its
   meaning and the reason. A wrong choice also shows what the chosen phrase
   means.
3. Press **Next situation** (or Enter). The score in the top right counts
   correct first choices.
4. After situation 10, press **See phrase sheet** to see all ten best
   phrases, with a check or a cross for each of your first choices.
5. **Restart** clears all answers.

## Lesson Plan

**Audience:** adult travelers preparing for the trip.  
**Duration:** 10 minutes.  
**Prerequisites:** basic Turkish phrases and their meanings, and the Turkish
pronunciation of c, ç, ş, ğ and ı.  
**Learning objective (Bloom: Apply):** Use the appropriate Turkish phrase in
ten everyday situations from our Istanbul days, choosing the best phrase in
at least 8 of 10 situations on the first attempt.

### Activities

1. **Say it first (2 min):** Before choosing in each situation, say all three
   phrases aloud. The ı in *Günaydın* and *Hayır* is the "uh" sound, and ş in
   *Teşekkür* is "sh."
2. **Choose (5 min):** Work through all ten situations. For each phrase,
   ask: who says this, and to whom?
3. **Find the pattern (3 min):** On the phrase sheet, look at the situations
   you missed. How many of your wrong choices were phrases that the other
   person in the exchange would say?

### Assessment

Mastery is 8 of 10 correct on the first attempt. A follow-up question: *On
the evening of Day 1, a waiter brings your balık ekmek and says* Afiyet
olsun. *What could you say when you hand back the empty plate to the cook?*
*Elinize sağlık*, "health to your hands," or a simple *Teşekkür ederim*.
