## About This MicroSim

The old town of Heraklion, Venetian Candia, is small enough to walk in an
hour, but its landmarks are easy to misplace. This map quiz asks us to find
eight of them before we arrive. The base map shows streets, the harbor, and
the shoreline, but no place names, so the map cannot give the answers away.

Eight cards come in a fixed order: Lions Square with the Morosini Fountain,
the Loggia, the Church of Agios Titos, the Venetian arsenal, Koules Fortress,
the Archaeological Museum, the Martinengo Bastion with the grave of Nikos
Kazantzakis, and the Pantocrator (Chania) Gate. For each card we click where
we think the landmark stands. A click within 150 m of the landmark counts as
correct. Each card allows two tries, and only the first try counts toward the
score.

Three cards target common mix-ups:

- **Koules Fortress** stands out at the end of the harbor breakwater, not on
  the town's shore.
- **The Venetian walls** ring the town on its landward side, not along the
  harbor, so the Martinengo Bastion and the Pantocrator Gate lie on the
  town's upper, southern and western edges.
- **The Venetian civic center** is compact: the fountain, the Loggia, and Agios
  Titos stand within about 200 m of one another.

After card 8, all eight markers stay on the map and a dashed blue line joins
the stops of our own walk on Day 9: the museum, the Lions, the Loggia, Agios
Titos, the arsenal, and the Koules.

**Reference points.** Each reference point was checked against OpenStreetMap
in November 2026. Six of the specification's points were within 30 m of the
OpenStreetMap feature and were only rounded. Two were corrected:

| Card | Specification | Used here | Correction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7. Martinengo Bastion and Kazantzakis's grave | 35.3317, 25.1311 | 35.3327, 25.1307 | About 110 m, moved to the grave on top of the bastion |
| 8. Pantocrator (Chania) Gate | 35.3378, 25.1255 | 35.3370, 25.1249 | About 100 m, moved to the gate itself |

## How to Use

1. Read the card below the map, for example *Lions Square: the Morosini
   Fountain and the Basilica of San Marco. Where would you find the Lions?*
2. Click the map where you think the landmark stands. A green ring means
   within 150 m; a red ring means a miss.
3. After a first miss, the sim tells you how far away you were. Click again.
   After a correct answer or a second miss, the landmark's marker appears, a
   dashed line shows the distance from your click, and a note opens with the
   landmark's era and dates.
4. Press **Next card** (or Enter). The score in the top right counts correct
   first tries.
5. After card 8, press **See result** to show all eight markers and the line
   of our walk. Click any marker to read its note again.
6. Zoom with **+** and **−** and drag to pan. The mouse wheel scrolls the page,
   not the map. **Try Again** clears the map and the score.

## Lesson Plan

**Audience:** adult travelers preparing for the trip.  
**Duration:** 10 minutes.  
**Prerequisites:** Candia, the Morosini Fountain, the Loggia, the Church of
Agios Titos, the Venetian arsenal, Koules Fortress, the Venetian walls and
bastions, the Martinengo Bastion, the Pantocrator (Chania) Gate, and the
Heraklion Archaeological Museum (Chapter 13).  
**Learning objective (Bloom: Remember):** Locate eight landmarks of Venetian
and Ottoman Heraklion on a street map of the old town, placing at least six of
the eight within 150 m of the true position on the first attempt.

### Activities

1. **Find the edges (2 min):** Before answering, find the old harbor and the
   long breakwater that encloses it, then trace the ring of streets and parks
   that follows the line of the walls around the town's landward side.
2. **Locate (6 min):** Work through all eight cards. Before each click, say
   which part of the town the landmark belongs to: the civic center, the
   harbor, or the walls.
3. **Read the walk (2 min):** On the result map, follow the blue line from the
   museum to the Koules. Notice that cards 1 to 5 lie on one short downhill
   route to the sea, less than a kilometer from the Lions to the Koules,
   while the walls ring the town's upper edge.

### Assessment

Mastery is 6 of 8 correct on the first attempt. As a follow-up, ask: *We have
one hour in the old town and want to see the Venetian civic center and the
sea fortress. Which way do we walk from the Lions, and roughly how far?* A
strong answer says north, downhill along 25th August Street, past the Loggia
and Agios Titos to the harbor, less than a kilometer.
