## About This MicroSim

Rhodes became part of Greece only after World War II. Before that it was
Ottoman for almost four centuries, Italian for three decades, German for
twenty months, and British for almost two years. This timeline puts those
rulers in one row and thirteen events in the row below, so the question
"who governed Rhodes?" can be answered for any date.

| Span | Power governing Rhodes |
|---|---|
| 1522 to May 1912 | Ottoman Empire |
| May 1912 to September 1943 | Italy |
| September 1943 to May 8, 1945 | Germany (military occupation) |
| May 8, 1945 to March 31, 1947 | Britain (military administration) |
| From March 31, 1947 | Greece (formal union March 7, 1948) |

Each event's number is colored by the power that governed Rhodes on that
date. Three events, Ochi Day in 1940, the fall of Athens in 1941, and its
liberation in 1944, happened on the Greek mainland; their hollow numbers
show at a glance that Athens and Rhodes were on opposite sides of the war
until 1945. Opening any event names the power that governed the island at
that moment.

The timeline includes the deportation of the Jews of Rhodes on July 23,
1944. It is shown in the same plain style as every other event, with its
date and the number deported, because the date is what the third question
depends on: the deportation took place under German control, ten months
after the Germans took the island from the Italians and less than a year
before the war in Europe ended.

## How to Use

1. Read the prompt in the yellow bar, then compare the two rows from 1910
   to 1949.
2. Press **Next event** to read the events in date order, or click any
   event. Hover over an event or a colored span for its dates.
3. Press **Close-up: 1936–1949** to spread out the crowded war years, and
   **Full span** to return. **◀ Earlier** and **Later ▶** move the view;
   you can also drag the timeline sideways.
4. Answer the five questions on the right. Each is scored once. The correct
   answer and its reason are shown, and the spans and events the question
   depends on are outlined in yellow on the timeline.
5. **Try Again** clears the five questions and starts again.

## Lesson Plan

**Audience:** adult travelers preparing for Day 8 on Rhodes.  
**Duration:** 10–15 minutes.  
**Prerequisites:** Ottoman Rhodes, Italian rule of Rhodes, World War II,
the Rhodes deportation of 1944, Dodecanese union with Greece, and the
1923 population exchange from Chapter 7.  
**Learning objective (Bloom: Understand):** Interpret a timeline of the
powers that governed Rhodes from 1912 to 1948 to answer which power
governed the island at a given date and what followed from it, with at
least 4 of 5 questions correct on the first attempt.

### Activities

1. **Read the bands (3 min):** Name the five powers in order and estimate
   how long each governed the island.
2. **Step through the events (4 min):** Press **Next event** thirteen times.
   At each event, say which power governed Rhodes and whether the event
   happened on Rhodes or on the mainland.
3. **Answer the questions (5 min):** For each question, find the date on
   the timeline before choosing an answer.
4. **Compare Athens and Rhodes (3 min):** Using events 6, 7, 8 and 10,
   explain to a travel companion why October 1944 meant liberation in
   Athens but not on Rhodes.

### Assessment

Mastery is 4 of 5 questions correct on the first attempt. Follow-up
question: *Why did the Muslims of Rhodes stay when the Muslims of Crete were
exchanged in the 1920s?* Because the 1923 exchange applied only to Greece
and Turkey, and Rhodes had been Italian since 1912.
