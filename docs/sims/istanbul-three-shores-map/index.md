## About This MicroSim

Istanbul is split by two bodies of water into three parts. The Bosphorus
divides Europe from Asia, and the Golden Horn, a curved inlet off the
Bosphorus, divides the European side in two. That leaves three shores:

| Shore | Where it is | What stands there |
|---|---|---|
| Historic peninsula | European side, south of the Golden Horn | Greek Byzantium, Constantine's city, and the early Ottoman capital |
| Galata | European side, north of the Golden Horn | The Genoese merchants' colony and its tower |
| Asian shore | East of the Bosphorus | Kadıköy (ancient Chalcedon) and Üsküdar (ancient Chrysopolis) |

The map starts with only the three shores and three bodies of water labeled.
The satellite imagery carries no place names, so the map cannot give away the
answers. Twelve places are asked in a fixed order. We choose a shore first,
and only then does the place's dot appear at its true location, colored by
its shore, with a short note about it.

Two places target common mix-ups. Galata Tower stands across the Golden Horn
from the old city, but both are in Europe; the Golden Horn is an inlet, not
the strait. Rumeli Hisarı, Mehmed II's 1452 fortress, stands far up the
Bosphorus on the European shore; for this quiz, all of the European side
north of the Golden Horn counts as Galata.

## How to Use

1. Read the question below the map, for example *Sarayburnu (Seraglio
   Point): which shore?* Each place has one attempt.
2. Press **Historic peninsula**, **Galata**, or **Asian shore** (or press 1, 2
   or 3). The correct shore turns green and a wrong choice turns red.
3. The place's dot appears on the map with a note about it. When the place
   lies outside the current view, the map widens to show it.
4. Press **Next place** (or Enter). The score in the top right counts correct
   first answers.
5. After place 12, press **See result**. Every dot is shown together. Click
   any dot to read its note again, zoom with **+** and **−**, and drag to pan;
   the mouse wheel scrolls the page instead of the map.
6. **Restart** hides the dots and clears the score.

## Lesson Plan

**Audience:** adult travelers preparing for the trip.  
**Duration:** 10 minutes.  
**Prerequisites:** strait, the Bosphorus Strait, the historic peninsula,
Galata, the Asian shore, and the Golden Horn.  
**Learning objective (Bloom: Understand):** Classify twelve Istanbul places by
the shore they stand on (the historic peninsula, Galata, or the Asian shore),
with at least 10 of 12 correct on the first attempt.

### Activities

1. **Find the water (2 min):** Before answering, trace the Bosphorus from the
   top of the map to the Sea of Marmara, then trace the Golden Horn from its
   mouth to the northwest. Say which body of water separates which two shores.
2. **Classify (5 min):** Work through all twelve places. For each, say aloud
   what you know about the place (Greek, Byzantine, Ottoman, Genoese, or a
   ferry town) before choosing.
3. **Read the pattern (3 min):** On the result map, notice that places 1 to 7,
   everything Byzantine and early Ottoman, sit on the historic peninsula,
   while the Genoese tower and the Asian-side towns sit across the water from
   it. Find the two gates of the land walls and notice that the walls cross
   the peninsula from water to water, rather than running along the Bosphorus.

### Assessment

Mastery is 10 of 12 correct on the first attempt. As a follow-up, ask: *We
take the ferry from Eminönü to Kadıköy on the evening of Day 1. Which body of
water do we cross, and which continent are we on when we get off?* A strong
answer names the Bosphorus (near its mouth on the Sea of Marmara) and Asia.
