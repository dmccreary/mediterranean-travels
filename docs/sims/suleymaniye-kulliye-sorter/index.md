# What Did the Külliye Do?

## About This MicroSim

The Süleymaniye Mosque, built for Suleiman the Magnificent by Mimar Sinan
between 1550 and 1557, is only the center of a much larger plan. Around it
stand the buildings of its **külliye**, a complex of public services run as a
single charitable endowment (*vakıf*). This MicroSim takes twelve of those
buildings, one card at a time, and asks which public need each one served:

| Need | What it means |
|---|---|
| 1. Worship | Buildings for prayer |
| 2. Learning | Buildings for teaching and study |
| 3. Health | Buildings for treating the sick |
| 4. Food and shelter | Buildings that fed or housed people |
| 5. Income | Property whose rent or fees paid for the foundation |
| 6. Remembrance | Tombs of the founder and family |

Two cards are traps. The **medical school** sounds like health, but it
trained physicians; the patients were treated in the hospital next door. The
**public bath** sounds like a free service, but bathers paid a fee, and the
fee went to the foundation. Those two cards, with the rows of shops, show the
idea at the heart of the vakıf: some buildings existed only to pay for the
rest.

When all twelve are sorted, the summary reads Worship 1, Learning 4, Health 1,
Food and shelter 2, Income 2, Remembrance 2. Four of the twelve served
learning, the largest group.

## How to Use

1. Read the six needs in the colored column headers.
2. Read the building on the card in the white panel and press the button for
   the need it served (or press 1 to 6). Each card has one attempt.
3. The correct column is outlined in green, a wrong choice in red, and the
   card joins its correct column. The feedback gives the reason. The number at
   the bottom of each column counts its buildings.
4. Press **Next card** to continue. The score in the top right counts correct
   first answers.
5. After the twelfth card, press **See result**. Hover over any card in the
   columns to read its reason again; missed cards have a red border.
6. **Try Again** repeats all twelve cards.

## Lesson Plan

**Audience:** adult travelers preparing for Day 3 in Istanbul.  
**Duration:** 10–15 minutes.  
**Prerequisites:** vakıf, vakfiye, külliye, medrese and imaret.  
**Learning objective (Bloom: Understand):** Classify twelve buildings of the
Süleymaniye külliye by the public need each one served (worship, learning,
health, food and shelter, income, or remembrance), with at least 10 of 12
correct on the first attempt.

### Activities

1. **Recall the engine (2 min):** Before starting, say in one sentence how a
   vakıf keeps paying for its services after its founder dies.
2. **Sort (6 min):** Work through all twelve cards. For each one, ask two
   questions before choosing: *who used this building?* and *who paid for
   it?*
3. **Read the summary (3 min):** On the result screen, count the buildings
   that spent money and the ones that earned it. Which group is larger, and
   why does the complex need both?
4. **Plan the walk (3 min):** Using the chapter's walk around the Süleymaniye,
   decide which of these buildings we can still see from the street on Day 3,
   and which one we can eat lunch in.

### Assessment

Mastery is 10 of 12 correct on the first attempt, including both the
medical school (card 5) and the public bath (card 9). A follow-up question:
*If an Ottoman founder endowed a mosque, a school and a soup kitchen but no
shops, baths or farmland, what would happen to them over the next fifty
years?* Without income-producing property, nothing would pay the teachers,
the cooks or the repairs once the founder's gift was spent.
