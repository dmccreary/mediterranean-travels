# Timeline of Events

[Chapter 1](../chapters/01-long-timeline/index.md) introduces the nine periods
of Eastern Mediterranean history on a timeline that shows the periods alone.
This appendix puts 29 events on our route onto the same to-scale line, from the
first palace at Knossos to the founding of the Republic of Turkey. Each event
names the chapter that covers it, so the timeline is also a map of the book in
time. Use it before a day's excursion to see where that day's sites fall, or
after the trip to tie the places back together.

## Interactive Timeline

The timeline opens with the nine period bands and a six-question quiz about
period lengths and the gaps between events. Events appear as the questions are
answered, and all 29 appear after the last one. Click any event to read about
it and follow the link to its chapter.
