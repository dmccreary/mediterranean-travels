# References: Day 9, Part 2: Venetian Heraklion and Crete Today

1. [Kingdom of Candia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Candia) - Wikipedia - Covers Venice's rule of Crete from 1205 to 1669, its government, its fortified capital at Candia, and its mixed Greek and Latin society. It is the main background for this chapter's Venetian walk.

2. [Siege of Candia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Candia) - Wikipedia - Tells the Ottoman siege of the city from 1648 to 1669, one of the longest in history, and its end in Ottoman rule. It supports this chapter's sections on the Venetian walls and Ottoman Crete.

3. [Aldus Manutius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aldus_Manutius) - Wikipedia - Describes the Venetian printer who published Greek classics, introduced italic type, and made small, portable editions. It supports this chapter's sections on Aldine editions and the Learning for All note on pocket classics.

4. *The World of Aldus Manutius: Business and Scholarship in Renaissance Venice* (1979) - Martin Lowry - Cornell University Press - Lowry treats Aldus as both scholar and businessman, showing how Greek exiles, money, and print shop labor produced the Aldine editions. He gives background for this chapter's Marcus Musurus and Aldus sections.

5. *Crete: The Battle and the Resistance* (1991) - Antony Beevor - John Murray - Beevor tells the 1941 German airborne invasion and the long Cretan resistance from both sides, drawing on intelligence records and veterans' accounts. He expands this chapter's Battle of Crete section.

6. [Historical Museum of Crete](https://www.historical-museum.gr/en/) - Society of Cretan Historical Studies - The Heraklion museum of Byzantine, Venetian, and Ottoman Crete, with works by El Greco and a room for Nikos Kazantzakis. It fits this chapter's walk through Venetian Candia.

7. [Nikos Kazantzakis Museum](https://www.kazantzaki.gr/en) - Nikos Kazantzakis Museum - The museum in the village of Myrtia devoted to the Cretan author of Zorba the Greek. Background for this chapter's sections on Kazantzakis and Cretan village life.

8. [The Aldine Press](https://exhibits.lib.byu.edu/aldine/aldWel.html) - Harold B. Lee Library, Brigham Young University - A digital version of the library's Aldine exhibition, covering Aldus's Greek and Latin editions, italic type, and small octavo books. It matches this chapter's folio, quarto, and octavo diagram.

9. [Saving the Greeks](https://grolierclub.omeka.net/exhibits/show/aldus-manutius/saving-the-greeks) - Grolier Club - A section of the exhibition "Aldus Manutius: A Legacy More Lasting Than Bronze" on Aldus's Greek first editions, including those edited by the Cretan scholar Marcus Musurus. It connects to this chapter's Cretan scholars.

10. [Diet Review: Mediterranean Diet](https://nutritionsource.hsph.harvard.edu/healthy-weight/diet-reviews/mediterranean-diet/) - The Nutrition Source, Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health - A clear review of what the Mediterranean diet includes and what research shows about its health effects. It connects to this chapter's Cretan diet, horta, and dakos.
