# Day 5: The End of Empire and the Birth of Modern Turkey

## Summary

Day 5 (Sunday, November 15) is our last day in Istanbul, with the ship sailing at 6:00 PM. The chapter traces the empire's decline and the Greek War of Independence, then World War I and the Gallipoli campaign that made Mustafa Kemal's name. It follows the Greco-Turkish War and the Turkish War of Independence to the Republic of Turkey, Atatürk's reforms, secularism, and the debate over Hagia Sophia's status. Its Learning for All note tells the story of the 1928 alphabet reform and the Nation's Schools, including what the reform cost and how uncertain the old literacy figures are.
