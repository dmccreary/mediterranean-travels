# Going Home: Learning With Communities, Not To Them

## Summary

Read this short closing chapter on the way home. It gathers the Learning for All notes from every stop and weighs them together: the cost of delaying a knowledge technology, scribal resistance, the costs of top-down reform, and the shadow side of evangelism. It argues that democratizing education works best when it is done with communities, in their own languages, using mother-tongue learning and AI translation, and links back to AI-Native Schools. It ends with a prompt to write our own short 'good news' message about AI and education from one story we actually saw.
