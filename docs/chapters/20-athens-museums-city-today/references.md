# References: Day 13: Athens, Museums, Tutors, and the City Today

1. [Antikythera mechanism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antikythera_mechanism) - Wikipedia - Explains the ancient Greek geared device found in a shipwreck that predicted eclipses and planetary positions. It is the main background for this chapter's section on an ancient prediction machine.

2. [National Archaeological Museum, Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Archaeological_Museum,_Athens) - Wikipedia - Describes Greece's largest archaeological museum and its collections, from Mycenaean gold to bronze statues. It supports this chapter's morning at the museum.

3. [Bloom's 2 sigma problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloom%27s_2_sigma_problem) - Wikipedia - Summarizes Benjamin Bloom's 1984 finding that one-on-one tutoring with mastery learning raised average students far above classroom peers. It supports this chapter's sections on tutoring and mastery learning.

4. *Decoding the Heavens: A 2,000-Year-Old Computer and the Century-Long Search to Discover Its Secrets* (2008) - Jo Marchant - William Heinemann - Marchant tells the hunt to understand the Antikythera mechanism as a detective story of sponge divers, X-rays, and rival scholars. Her account brings this chapter's bronze machine to life.

5. *Brave New Words: How AI Will Revolutionize Education (and Why That's a Good Thing)* (2024) - Salman Khan - Viking - Khan argues that AI can give every student a patient personal tutor, the goal behind Bloom's 2 sigma finding, while keeping teachers central. His case fits this chapter's AI tutoring and over-reliance sections.

6. [National Archaeological Museum](https://www.namuseum.gr/en/) - National Archaeological Museum, Athens - The museum's official site, with collections, exhibitions, and visiting details. Use it to plan this chapter's visit and find the Antikythera mechanism gallery.

7. [A Model of the Cosmos in the Ancient Greek Antikythera Mechanism](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-021-84310-w) - Scientific Reports - A 2021 open-access study by a University College London team that rebuilds the gearing on the mechanism's front. It shows how researchers model the ancient prediction machine in this chapter.

8. [Lykeion Archaeological Site](http://odysseus.culture.gr/h/3/eh351.jsp?obj_id=20744) - Odysseus, Hellenic Ministry of Culture - The official page on the remains of the Lyceum, the school where Aristotle taught. It supports this chapter's section on Hadrian's temple and Aristotle's school.

9. [Plato, Phaedrus, Section 274](http://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus:text:1999.01.0174:text=Phaedrus:page=274) - Perseus Digital Library, Tufts University - Socrates tells the myth of Theuth and Thamus, in which a king warns that writing will weaken memory. The primary source for this chapter's section on Phaedrus and fear of new learning technology.

10. [AI Tutoring Outperforms In-Class Active Learning](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-97652-6) - Scientific Reports - A 2025 randomized trial in a Harvard physics course where students using a carefully designed AI tutor learned more in less time. Recent evidence for this chapter's AI tutoring discussion.
