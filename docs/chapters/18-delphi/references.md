# References: Day 12: Delphi

1. [Delphi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delphi) - Wikipedia - Covers the sanctuary on Mount Parnassus, its oracle, its monuments, its games, and its excavation by the French School. It is the main background for this chapter's walk up the site.

2. [Charioteer of Delphi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charioteer_of_Delphi) - Wikipedia - Describes the bronze statue of a chariot driver made about 470 BCE, how it survived, and what it shows about early Classical sculpture. It supports this chapter's museum section.

3. [Serpent Column](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serpent_Column) - Wikipedia - Tells the story of the bronze victory monument set up at Delphi after the Battle of Plataea and later moved to Constantinople's Hippodrome. It matches this chapter's "Two Ends of the Serpent Column" diagram.

4. *Delphi: A History of the Center of the Ancient World* (2014) - Michael Scott - Princeton University Press - Scott tells Delphi's history through the monuments and gifts that cities set up there, and ends with a guide for visitors. He explains why treasuries and victory monuments crowd this chapter's Sacred Way.

5. *Ancient Greek Athletics* (2004) - Stephen G. Miller - Yale University Press - Miller, who excavated the games site at Nemea, explains Greek athletics from the athletes' point of view, covering events, training, and the crown games. Background for this chapter's Pythian Games and stadium.

6. [The Archaeological Site of Delphi](https://delphi.culture.gr/) - Museum of Delphi, Hellenic Ministry of Culture - The official site for the sanctuary and museum, with plans, monuments, and visiting details. Use it to plan this chapter's walk from the Castalian Spring to the stadium.

7. [Selected Exhibits](https://delphi.culture.gr/museum/selected-exhibits/) - Museum of Delphi, Hellenic Ministry of Culture - The museum's guide to its highlights, including the Charioteer, the kouroi, and the gold and ivory statues. It supports this chapter's section on the Delphi Archaeological Museum.

8. [Delphi](http://odysseus.culture.gr/h/3/eh351.jsp?obj_id=2507) - Odysseus, Hellenic Ministry of Culture - The official page on the archaeological site, with its history and its monuments. It supports this chapter's sections on the treasuries, the theater, and the Athena Pronaia sanctuary.

9. [Delphes](https://www.efa.gr/delphes/) - École française d'Athènes - The French School at Athens, which has excavated Delphi since 1892, describes its work there. The page is in French, but it supports this chapter's section on the French excavation of Delphi.

10. [Pausanias, Description of Greece, Book 10, Chapter 9](http://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus:text:1999.01.0160:book=10:chapter=9) - Perseus Digital Library, Tufts University - The second-century CE traveler Pausanias walks up the Sacred Way and lists the offerings he saw. It lets readers follow this chapter's route with an ancient guidebook.
