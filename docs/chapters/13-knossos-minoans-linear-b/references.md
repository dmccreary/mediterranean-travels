# References: Day 9, Part 1: Knossos, the Minoans, and Linear B

1. [Knossos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knossos) - Wikipedia - Covers the Bronze Age palace, its layout, its excavation by Arthur Evans, and the debate over his reconstructions. It is the main background for this chapter's morning at Knossos.

2. [Minoan civilization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minoan_civilization) - Wikipedia - Describes the Bronze Age culture of Crete, its palaces, art, religion, trade, and decline, including the Thera eruption. It supports this chapter's sections on the palace economy, frescoes, and the bull-leaper.

3. [Linear B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_B) - Wikipedia - Explains the syllabic script used to write early Greek, its tablets, and how Michael Ventris deciphered it in 1952. It supports this chapter's Learning for All section on Kober, Ventris, and the decipherment.

4. *The Riddle of the Labyrinth: The Quest to Crack an Ancient Code* (2013) - Margalit Fox - Ecco - Fox retells the decipherment of Linear B through Alice Kober's index cards, showing the method step by step before Ventris's breakthrough. Her account matches this chapter's "Crack Linear B Like Ventris" activity.

5. *Knossos and the Prophets of Modernism* (2009) - Cathy Gere - University of Chicago Press - Gere shows how Arthur Evans's concrete reconstructions turned Knossos into a modern dream of a peaceful, goddess-loving Bronze Age. She helps answer this chapter's question: Minoan, Evans, or copy?

6. [Knossos](http://odysseus.culture.gr/h/3/eh351.jsp?obj_id=2369) - Odysseus, Hellenic Ministry of Culture - The official page on the Knossos site, with its history, monuments, and visiting details. Read it before this chapter's walk through the palace and the throne room.

7. [Heraklion Archaeological Museum](https://heraklionmuseum.gr/) - Heraklion Archaeological Museum - The official site of the museum that holds the Snake Goddess figurines, the Phaistos Disc, the bull-leaping fresco, and Linear A and B tablets. It supports this chapter's museum section.

8. [Program in Aegean Scripts and Prehistory](https://sites.utexas.edu/scripts/) - University of Texas at Austin - The research program that holds Alice Kober's papers and studies Linear A and Linear B. It connects to this chapter's Learning for All section on Kober's index cards.

9. [Sir Arthur Evans Archive](https://sirarthurevans.ashmus.ox.ac.uk/introduction/index.html) - Ashmolean Museum, University of Oxford - The museum's online guide to Evans's notebooks, photographs, and finds from Knossos. It shows the excavation records behind the reconstructions discussed in this chapter.

10. [Knossos 2025 Project](https://www.bsa.ac.uk/2023/01/26/knossos-2025-project/) - British School at Athens - The school's page on rebuilding its Knossos research center and the Stratigraphical Museum that stores excavated finds. It shows that research at Knossos continues today.
