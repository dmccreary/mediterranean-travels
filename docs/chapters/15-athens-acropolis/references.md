# References: Day 10: Athens, the Acropolis, and the Persian Wars

1. [Acropolis of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acropolis_of_Athens) - Wikipedia - Describes the rock and its buildings, from the Propylaea and the Parthenon to the Erechtheion and the Temple of Athena Nike. It is the main background for this chapter's climb up the Acropolis.

2. [Parthenon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parthenon) - Wikipedia - Covers the temple's design, sculptures, and later lives as church, mosque, and ruin, plus the dispute over the marbles in London. It supports this chapter's sections on the Parthenon and its five lives.

3. [Greco-Persian Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greco-Persian_Wars) - Wikipedia - Tells the wars between the Persian Empire and the Greek city-states, from Marathon in 490 BCE to Salamis and beyond. It supports this chapter's section on the Persian Wars and Xerxes' road to Salamis.

4. *The Parthenon* (2002) - Mary Beard - Profile Books - Beard follows the building's whole life as temple, church, mosque, ruin, and symbol, and explains the marbles dispute evenhandedly. Her approach matches this chapter's "Parthenon's Five Lives" and "Agreed Fact or Position?" activities.

5. *Persian Fire: The First World Empire and the Battle for the West* (2005) - Tom Holland - Little, Brown - Holland begins with the rise of Persia before turning to the Greeks, so Marathon and Salamis read as a clash seen from both sides. His account pairs with this chapter's use of Herodotus.

6. [Acropolis of Athens](http://odysseus.culture.gr/h/3/eh351.jsp?obj_id=2384) - Odysseus, Hellenic Ministry of Culture - The official page on the Acropolis, with its history, its monuments, and visiting details. Read it before this chapter's walk through the Propylaea to the Parthenon.

7. [Acropolis Museum](http://odysseus.culture.gr/h/1/eh151.jsp?obj_id=16781) - Odysseus, Hellenic Ministry of Culture - The official page on the museum at the foot of the rock, home to the Caryatids and the Parthenon sculptures kept in Athens. It supports this chapter's Acropolis Museum section.

8. [Acropolis Restoration Service](https://www.ysma.gr/en/) - Acropolis Restoration Service (YSMA) - The team restoring the Acropolis monuments shares its methods, projects, and educational materials. It shows how the Parthenon is being repaired today, the latest of the later lives in this chapter.

9. [Herodotus, The Histories, Book 8, Chapter 83](http://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus:text:1999.01.0126:book=8:chapter=83) - Perseus Digital Library, Tufts University - Herodotus's account of the morning of the Battle of Salamis, in English with the Greek text. Read the source this chapter relies on for Themistocles and Xerxes' defeat.

10. [Plutarch, Pericles, Chapter 13](http://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Plut.%20Per.%2013) - Perseus Digital Library, Tufts University - Plutarch describes Pericles's building program on the Acropolis and Phidias's role in overseeing it. A primary source for this chapter's Pericles, Phidias, and Parthenon sections.
